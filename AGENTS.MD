# mybudget

* A simple bugeting app for managing personal finances.
* Single page HTML 5 app.

## Features
* Enter regular and one-off income.
* Enter regular and one-off expenses.
* Balance tracking.
* Prediction of future balance based on current income and expenses.
* Table view
* Graph view

## Design
* Simple and intuitive interface.
* Colors: white, dark green, light green, and black.
* Responsive design.
